Our Mission

Democratising Career Growth
Across Africa

Joggle Jobs was founded in 2023 from Ogba, Lagos with a mission to close the information gap in Nigeria's job market. We noticed that talented professionals were missing out on top opportunities simply because they didn't know where to look.

Today, we aggregate listings from Nigeria's leading employers — including tech unicorns, banks, multinationals, and startups — alongside international companies actively seeking African talent. Our platform is fast, mobile-first, and always up to date.
